How to make a character!

Should be working

- Copy the Sonic folder and name it accordingly;
- Edit data in the folder;
- Search for 'CHAR ADD STUFF' in sonic.asm, _incObj/Sonic LoadGfx.asm, _incObj/Sonic Animate.asm & _inc/Palette Pointers.asm (optional) and add your character.

*You can delete things that are just gonna be copies in your folder and not do the macro related to them

- Add 1 to CharCount at the start of sonic.asm :D

PLEASE DO NOT ALTER SONIC AT ANY COST.

Porting GHM3 chars to 4:
- make sure to reorder the animations, ones not listed in the anim constants go after

ALSO PRESS A ON THE TITLE SCREEN TO SCROLL THROUGH CHARACTERS
